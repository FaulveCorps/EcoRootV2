# EcoRoot — Content Blueprint (MVP + v1)

This blueprint maps the MVP content into modules, lessons, and quizzes.

## Module 1: Foundations
**Goal:** Build core understanding of soil and why it matters.
- Lesson 1.1: What is Soil?
- Lesson 1.2: Soil Functions (food, water, habitats)
- Lesson 1.3: How Soil Gets Contaminated
- Lesson 1.4: Common Pollutants (heavy metals, chemicals)

**Quiz Focus:** definitions, sources, basic impacts

## Module 2: Pollution Sources
**Goal:** Identify major human activities that lead to soil pollution.
- Lesson 2.1: Industrial Waste and Spills
- Lesson 2.2: Agricultural Runoff and Pesticides
- Lesson 2.3: Improper Waste Disposal
- Lesson 2.4: Mining and Construction

**Quiz Focus:** source recognition and examples

## Module 3: Impacts
**Goal:** Understand real‑world consequences.
- Lesson 3.1: Health Impacts
- Lesson 3.2: Food Safety and Agriculture
- Lesson 3.3: Ecosystem Damage
- Lesson 3.4: Water System Contamination

**Quiz Focus:** cause‑effect relationships

## Module 4: Solutions (v1)
**Goal:** Explore prevention and cleanup strategies.
- Lesson 4.1: Prevention First
- Lesson 4.2: Cleaning Contaminated Soil
- Lesson 4.3: Policy and Community Action

**Quiz Focus:** prevention, remediation, policy

## Glossary (Seed List)
- Soil contamination
- Heavy metals
- Pesticide residue
- Leaching
- Remediation
- Bioaccumulation

## Teacher Resources (v1)
- Discussion prompts
- Mini activities
- Local case study guidance
- Reflection prompts

## Quiz Guidelines
- 3–5 questions per lesson
- Mix of multiple choice and true/false
- Simple, age‑appropriate language
- Provide short feedback for each answer
